About Me

I’m Vivash Singh — a multimedia designer, content creator and web developer from Fiji. I make visuals and digital experiences that feel cinematic, considered and impossible to scroll past.

Photomanipulation is where I made my name. My compositing work has been recognised by Adobe and by creative communities around the world, and I’m known today as one of Fiji’s leading photomanipulation artists.

That same eye shapes everything I create — cinematic compositing, colour grading, light VFX and motion design — for brands including Mitsubishi Motors, Apple and Coca-Cola, across branding, campaigns and digital production.

I also design and build websites, pairing clean, responsive interfaces with the visual craft of my design work, so they look as good as they perform.

My goal is simple: to put Fiji on the global creative stage, one piece of work at a time.
